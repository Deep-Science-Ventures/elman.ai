How it works

Novelty judged against the evidence.

Elman searches the relevant prior art, then assesses each element of the invention for novelty and defensibility against what it finds. Every judgement resolves to the specific prior-art passage it turns on — so a patent attorney can see exactly why a claim is or isn't clear, and act on it.

It shares the same auditable foundation as Elman FTO: per-element decomposition, one isolated assessment per reference, and a conclusion that traces to a source you can open rather than a score you have to trust.

Element-level novelty

Each element of the invention is assessed independently against the prior art, not scored in aggregate.

Evidence on every call

Each judgement links to the specific prior-art passage it rests on — the basis for filing strategy you can defend.

Strategy-ready

See what's clear to file, what needs narrowing, and where the white space is — grounded in the references themselves.
