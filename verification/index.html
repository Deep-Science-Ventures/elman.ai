How it works

Source-span resolution on every claim.

A language model that answers from memorised training data cannot tell you where a claim comes from — and in drug development, an answer you can't trace is an answer you can't use. Elman Verification does the opposite of recalling: it gathers the evidence, reads it, and binds every conclusion to the exact passage that supports or refutes it.

That means a reviewer never has to take the verdict on trust. They open the cited sentence, trial arm, or figure and check it themselves — the assessment is inspectable, not asserted.

Source-span resolution

Every claim is bound to a specific paper sentence, trial arm, or figure — the precise evidence behind the call, not a citation to a whole document.

Audit-grade depth

Hundreds to thousands of papers and in-silico runs assessed per claim, with provenance specific to pharmaceutical evidence.

No black-box verdicts

No memorised priors and no averaged-out signal — conclusions trace to evidence you can read, so the reasoning survives scrutiny.

Who it's for

For decisions that must withstand scrutiny.

Verification serves scientific diligence, target rationale review, and any decision where a claim has to hold up to an expert who will check the source. Investors running scientific diligence, R&D teams pressure-testing a hypothesis, and reviewers auditing a body of evidence get conclusions they can defend line by line — built from the literature and in-silico runs, with the trail attached.
