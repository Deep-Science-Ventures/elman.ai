How it works

Analysis at scale, with the provenance intact.

Elman analyses cohort and omics data directly — not summaries of it — running the pipelines needed to read a dataset at single-cell resolution. Findings carry pharma-specific provenance: you can trace a result back to the data and the steps that produced it, rather than taking an aggregate on faith.

This is the analytical engine behind several use-cases in the suite — it underpins Verification audits and composes with Hypothesis Generation for target identification.

Single-cell resolution

Traceable provenance

Who it's for

For computational biology and translational research.

Bioinformatics-at-scale serves computational biologists and translational teams who need to interrogate cohort data without waiting on a queue — and to hand downstream reviewers a result with its provenance attached. It powers Elman's deeper use-cases, from verification audits to biomarker discovery via scoped pilots.
