How it works

Hypotheses you can act on, not just read.

A generative model will happily invent plausible-sounding hypotheses with nothing underneath them. Elman does the opposite: it reads the dataset and the surrounding literature, then proposes hypotheses that are grounded in specific evidence — and shows you that evidence alongside each one.

Because every hypothesis arrives with its supporting trail, it flows straight into the rest of the suite: hand it to Verification to pressure-test against the evidence, or compose it with bioinformatics for full target identification.

Grounded, not invented

Each hypothesis is tied to the data and literature that motivate it — no free-floating speculation.

Evidence attached

The supporting trail travels with every hypothesis, so a scientist can judge it on the spot.

Composes downstream

Feeds directly into Verification and Bioinformatics-at-scale for target identification and beyond.

Who it's for

For target discovery and translational teams.

Hypothesis Generation serves discovery scientists and translational researchers who need to turn a fresh dataset into a prioritised, defensible set of leads. Elman gets them to candidate hypotheses faster — with the reasoning attached, so the shortlist survives review.
